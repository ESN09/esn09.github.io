Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA Shorts Publisher ("the app") is operated by the owner of the TikTok account @fan2rp and the YouTube channel @fan_2_rp for personal use. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account. YouTube: permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and schedule publication on the connected channel.

How the data is used

Only to show which account videos are posted to and to publish the owner's own video files with the captions and settings the owner approved. The app does not read, collect or analyse other users' data, messages, followers or viewing activity.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold or shared with third parties, and no data is sent anywhere other than TikTok's and Google's official APIs.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings, Security, Apps and services) or in the Google account (Security, Third-party access).

Contact

Through the YouTube channel @fan_2_rp.
