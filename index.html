GTA Shorts Publisher

GTA Shorts Publisher is a personal desktop tool that schedules and publishes the owner's own short videos (GTA 5 vs GTA 6 gameplay comparisons with voice-over) to the owner's TikTok account @fan2rp and YouTube channel FAN2RP.

How it works

The creator signs in with TikTok once. Before each video is posted, the creator reviews it on the app's approval page: preview, caption, who can view it, comment/duet/stitch settings and commercial content disclosure. At the scheduled time the app posts the approved video with exactly those settings.

The app is not offered to the public and only publishes to accounts its owner controls.
